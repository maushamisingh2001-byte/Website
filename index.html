Got a minute? Read this first.[cite: 1]

Is my LinkedIn or Naukri password safe?[cite: 1]

Yes. The extension runs inside your Chrome browser where you are already logged in. Your credentials never leave your browser, and we never ask for them.[cite: 1]

Will the resume I build pass an ATS?[cite: 1]

Yes. By avoiding complex formatting and using your exact facts mapped cleanly to the job requirements, the exported PDF is highly ATS-friendly.[cite: 1]

What does the match score mean?[cite: 1]

It indicates how well your profile aligns with the required skills and experience for that specific job posting, helping you focus your time on high-probability applications.[cite: 1]

Do I need a laptop?[cite: 1]

For searching and applying, the extension needs a desktop Chrome or Edge browser. However, you can check results, match scores, and download resumes on your phone.[cite: 1]

What happens when my plan ends?[cite: 1]

You revert to the free plan. Nothing changes automatically, and your data stays secure. You only pay when you choose to top up.[cite: 1]
