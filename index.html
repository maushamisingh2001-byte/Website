Before you sign up

Something else? Write to us.

Is my LinkedIn or Naukri password safe?
Yes. The extension runs in your own Chrome, where you're already logged in. Your passwords never leave your browser, and we never ask for them.
Will the resume add things I haven't done?
No. Every line comes from your profile, your LinkedIn export, the resumes and notes you gave us, and skills you added yourself. You read and can edit the resume before you download it.
What does the match score mean?
It's how well your profile fits the job, from 0 to 100, with what matched and what's missing. It is not an "ATS score", and we don't claim to predict any company's hiring software.
Do I need a laptop?
For searching, yes. The extension needs Chrome on a laptop or desktop. You can check results, build resumes and download PDFs on your phone.
