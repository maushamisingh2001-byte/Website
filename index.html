FAQ

Is my LinkedIn or Naukri password safe?

Yes. The extension runs entirely inside your Chrome browser where you are already logged in. Your credentials never leave your browser, and we never ask for them.

Will the resume I build pass an ATS?

Yes. By avoiding complex formatting, columns, and graphics, and using your exact facts mapped cleanly to the job requirements, the exported PDF is highly ATS-friendly.

What does the match score actually mean?

It indicates how well your professional profile aligns with the required hard skills and experience for that specific job posting, helping you focus on high-probability applications.

Do I need a laptop to use this?

For searching jobs and applying, the extension requires a desktop Chrome or Edge browser. However, you can review match scores and download your resumes via mobile.

What happens when my paid plan ends?

Your account reverts to the free tier. Nothing changes automatically, and your data remains secure. You only pay again if you explicitly choose to top up.
